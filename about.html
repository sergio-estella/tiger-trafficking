About the data

Every number in Tiger Trafficking comes from two published sources: TRAFFIC’s Skin and Bones reports on tiger seizures, and the Global Tiger Forum’s 2023 estimate of wild tigers. This page explains what each figure means, how the charts were calculated and where the data has limits.

Definitions

Seizure
One law-enforcement event in which tigers or tiger parts were confiscated. A seizure can contain several kinds of parts and several tigers.
Tigers seized
Whole animals (dead or alive) plus the number of tigers the parts are estimated to come from. It is an estimate and a minimum.
Part appearance
A part counted once per seizure it appears in: a seizure with skins and claws adds one to skins and one to claws. The shares of parts are shares of all part appearances.
Parts
Skins, bones, skulls, teeth, claws, paws, meat, derivatives (products made from tiger parts), other parts (whiskers and others), dead tigers and live tigers.
Trophy and amulet
Trophy = skins + bones. Amulet = claws + teeth. Each as a share (%) of the part appearances of that year.
Whole animals
Share (%) of seizures that included dead tigers, live tigers, or both.
Tiger range countries
The 13 countries where wild tigers live or lived recently: Bangladesh, Bhutan, Cambodia, China, India, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Nepal, Russia, Thailand and Viet Nam. Seizures elsewhere are “outside the tiger range”.
Extinct in the wild
A tiger range country with no wild tigers in the 2023 estimate: Cambodia, Laos and Viet Nam.

How each chart is made

One face per year

The big head is a Voronoi diagram: one cell per kind of part, its area proportional to the number of seizures that contained it (January 2000 – June 2025). Below, one small head per year, its area proportional to the seizures of that year, its cells to that year’s mix of parts. Units seized (pieces, weights) in the legend cover January 2000 – June 2022.

The trafficking clock

One slice per year, 2000 to 2025, clockwise. Each ring is drawn to its own scale: wild tigers (census line, 2000, 2010, 2016, 2023), seizures and tigers seized (inside and outside the tiger range), the share of seizures outside the range, whole animals, trophy and amulet shares, and people jailed (2000 – 2021).

Year by year

The same year positions in three steps: seizures per year, tigers seized per year, and the share of seizures with whole animals. The strip below gives the share of seizures outside the tiger range. Seizures and tigers are kept apart on purpose: one counts events, the other animals.

What is trafficked

A radar of the share of each part among all part appearances, for five periods: 2000–04, 2005–09, 2010–14, 2015–19 and 2020 – June 2025. The radial scale is a square root, so small shares stay visible. The thin lines are single years.

Trophy vs amulet

Skins + bones against claws + teeth, as a share of the part appearances of each year.

People jailed for tiger trafficking

People jailed in tiger range countries, per year and country, 2000 – 2021 (375 in total). There is no yearly breakdown for 2022 – 2025.

Seizures recorded

One hexagon per tiger range country, placed roughly as on a map (not to scale). Green circle: wild tigers in 2023, area proportional to the number; dashed when there are none. Hatched fill: share of seizures with whole animals, 2020 – June 2025. Each thread runs from a year to a country; its width is the seizures of that country that year. Grey hexagons: seizures outside the tiger range, January 2000 – June 2025, without a yearly breakdown.

Where tigers still live

The map shows tiger range countries with wild tigers in 2023, those with none, and countries outside the range. Circles: wild tigers in 2023 (Global Tiger Forum). Country cards: seizures January 2000 – June 2025 (TRAFFIC).

2025: January to June

The 2025 figures cover only the first half of the year and are drawn dashed or marked with an asterisk. The yearly table has no 2025 totals, so they were worked out from the overall totals:

  • Seizures 2025 = 2,551 − seizures 2000–2024 = 33.
  • Seizures in tiger range countries 2025 = sum of the country figures for January – June 2025 = 30, so 3 outside the range.
  • Tigers seized 2025 = 3,808 − tigers seized 2000–2024 = 33, with no split between inside and outside the range.

Limits and discrepancies

  • Seizures are not trafficking. They show what was found, which depends on how hard authorities looked and how much they reported. More seizures can mean more enforcement, not more trade.
  • People jailed: 387 or 375. 387 covers January 2000 – June 2022; 375 is the total of the yearly breakdown by country, 2000 – 2021. They cover different periods and do not contradict each other; each is shown with its own period.
  • Units seized (1,313 skins, 11,528 bones, 654 dead tigers…) cover January 2000 – June 2022, while everything else runs to June 2025. Derivatives and skulls have no units.
  • Tigers seized includes tigers estimated from parts: a minimum, not a count.
  • Wild tigers: the country estimates of 2023 add up to 5,574, the figure used across the story (“about 5,600”).
  • Positions in the hexagon chart are approximate, not a map to scale.
